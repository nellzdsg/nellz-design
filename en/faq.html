How much does it cost?+
Avatar — $6, banner — $6, animated emoji — $1 each. Order three or more pieces in a single order and you get 20% off. The calculator on the services page does the maths.
How long does an order take?+
Usually 3–5 hours once the brief is clear. For a large order or a late-night request it's 24 hours at most. If you're in a hurry, just say so — speeding things up is almost always possible.
What should I send to get started?+
A few words about what you need and for which channel. Plus a reference if you have one — that helps me hit the mark. If you don't have one, I'll suggest a direction myself.
How many revisions are included?+
Two revisions per piece — free and within the chosen concept. I adjust composition, text and colours. If you want to change the whole idea, we'll discuss it separately — usually it's easy to sort out in chat.
Are there discounts?+
Yes: 20% off when you order three or more pieces. Every piece counts — an avatar, a banner and an emoji already make three. The discount applies automatically, there's no need to ask.
How does the referral programme work?+
Bring a friend and I'll return 50% of their order as credit. The credit can be exchanged for a free piece on orders from $10.
How do I pay?+
By card or cryptocurrency. The amount is fixed before work starts — there are no mid-project surcharges.
Which file formats do I get?+
Avatars and banners come as PNG. Animated emoji come as TGS (Telegram's format) or GIF if you need them elsewhere.
Do you take large orders — a full channel set?+
Yes. A complete set — avatar plus banner, an emoji pack, chat styling — is priced at the same fixed rates with the discount from three pieces. Tell me what you have in mind and I'll put together a quote.
