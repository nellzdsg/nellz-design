about

I'm nellz. I design visuals for Telegram.

For more than two years I've been creating avatars, banners and animated emoji — for small channels, established stores, gaming communities and personal blogs.

Along the way I've completed over 400 projects and collected more than 300 reviews, with 90% of orders closed on positive feedback. To me it's not just about making something pretty — it's about understanding who the channel is for and what it sells, which is why most clients approve the work from the first draft.

I also keep to my deadlines: a typical order is ready in 3–5 hours, and a complex one takes 24 hours at most. Revisions are part of the process — two of them are already included in the price.

Four things the result rests on

No surprises on price
